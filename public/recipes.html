Classic Garlic Butter Chicken

Advertisement Placeholder Above Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Cook chicken until golden and fully cooked.
  4. Add butter and garlic to the skillet.
  5. Spoon the garlic butter over the chicken for 1 to 2 minutes.
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.

Tips

Serve with rice, mashed potatoes, or roasted vegetables. You can also turn this into a lunch meal prep recipe.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 2
